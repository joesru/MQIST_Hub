## 📝 Objetivos y Resumen de la Materia

Esta asignatura proporciona al alumno los conceptos y técnicas básicas de operación de los sistemas de comunicaciones cuánticos, con especial énfasis en la construcción de canales de comunicaciones seguras y en el análisis de los protocolos en que se fundamentan. Se tratarán la distribución cuántica de claves, las diferentes posibilidades de implementación tecnológica y las técnicas de análisis de la seguridad de estos esquemas.

## 📚 Programa y Conceptos Clave (Keywords)

| **Bloque** | **Tema Principal** | **Keywords (Inglés)** |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Bloque 1** | **Introduction to cryptography:** Encryption and authentication of information, Classic symmetric key cryptography (One-time-pad scheme), Classic public-key and post-quantum cryptography. | [[Cryptography]], [[Symmetric Key]], [[Public-Key Cryptography]], [[Post-Quantum Cryptography]] |
| **Bloque 2** | **Quantum cryptography:** Quantum key distribution and security fundamentals. | [[Quantum Cryptography]], [[Quantum Key Distribution]] |
| **Bloque 3** | **Quantum key distribution protocols:** Prepare-and-measure protocols, Protocols based on entanglement and photonic interference, Protocols based on continuous variables, Data post-processing schemes. | [[Prepare-and-Measure Protocols]], [[Entanglement]], [[Photonic Interference]], [[Continuous Variables]] |
| **Bloque 4** | **Security of quantum key distribution protocols:** Individual, collective and coherent attacks. Asymptotic regime and finite regime. Security definition and Composability. | [[Security Fundamentals]], [[Quantum Attacks]], [[Asymptotic Regime]], [[Composability]] |
| **Bloque 5** | **Technological implementations:** Main experimental platforms. Limitations on the secret key generation rate. Photon-number-splitting attack. Decoy states. | [[Experimental Platforms]], [[Photon-Number-Splitting Attack]], [[Decoy States]] |
| **Bloque 6** | **Other quantum communication protocols:** Teleportation. Dense coding. Bit commitment. Quantum radar. | [[Quantum Teleportation]], [[Superdense Coding]], [[Bit Commitment]], [[Quantum Radar]] |
